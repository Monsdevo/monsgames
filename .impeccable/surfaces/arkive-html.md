# ARKIVE landing page

Mode: Persuade. Help prospective iPhone users understand what ARKIVE can do offline after setup and find it on the App Store through its official listing.

## Direction

**The Monolith** is the pinned direction for this page, drawn from the app’s `DESIGN.md`. Use a black canvas, warm off-white ink, graphite rules, and one full-width light section. Lead with “Knowledge Without a Signal.”. Set type in the app’s SF Pro through the system stack, with tight display headings and a small monospaced status line. Keep the two-column feature sections readable as a single column on phones.

Qualify offline chat with model setup, and Vision with a compatible Gemma 4 model and matching projector. Describe document import, offline search, category selection, and source references using the app’s shipped capabilities. Keep the app’s private product and design documents scoped to the app project and out of the published site.

## Assets and behavior

- The official Apple Marketing Tools badge and the footer link point to the published ARKIVE App Store listing. The layout uses typography instead of application imagery.
- Reveal the status line once, with a brief spectral glow per token that settles to muted ink. Reduced motion and JavaScript-free views show the complete, static line.
