# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is people discovering Bartu Yılmaz, also known as Mons, and his work. Secondary audiences include prospective players, collaborators, press, creators, and people seeking studio or game support.

## Product Purpose

This is Bartu Yılmaz / Mons's personal portfolio. The homepage introduces him first, then Mons Games as his independent game studio and Bad Haunts as a project. The existing detailed game presentation lives at bad-haunts.html.

## Positioning

Bartu Yılmaz / Mons is the person behind the work. Mons Games builds games around the stories people create together. Bad Haunts demonstrates that approach through asymmetric play where human investigators and player-controlled haunts perceive and shape the same match differently.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from social profiles, shared links, or search. They should understand the relationship between Bartu, Mons Games and Bad Haunts, explore the game, or contact Bartu without hunting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is an English-language, responsive static web experience with a small JavaScript enhancement layer.
- Only Mons Games and Bad Haunts are currently supplied as named portfolio projects. The user will provide more projects later; do not add placeholders, coming-soon cards, fabricated project names or inferred projects from repository folders.
- User-supplied experience: more than ten native iOS SaaS experiments; one Unreal Engine multiplayer game; an ongoing attempt to port Vita3K (a PlayStation Vita emulator) to iOS; advanced experience with vibe coding, AI agents and prompt engineering. Present experiments and the Vita3K attempt honestly, not as shipped products.
- Preserve English until the user asks for a language change. Do not invent age, location, employment history, awards, availability or additional social accounts.
- Production hosting is GitHub Pages at monsgames.net, published from Monsdevo/monsgames main. The Sites preview is separate.
- The site must retain working social, email, legal, advertising, and support destinations already present in the repository.
- Historical experiments and unrelated microsites are not studio portfolio content and must not be promoted from the homepage.
- Mustafi Takvimi must be removed from the source and deployment and must remain inaccessible even when its former URL is entered directly.
- Commercial claims, launch dates, platform availability, testimonials, and performance claims must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Lead with Bartu Yılmaz / Mons. Reuse the existing Mons Games mark and the condensed wordmark style from the masthead; retain the Mons Games studio name in its own section.
- Preserve the Bad Haunts name, supplied logo, asymmetric horror-comedy premise, and its established social accounts.
- Keep the voice direct, confident, and focused on interaction, authorship, and memorable player stories.
- A redesign may replace the incumbent visual system but must not replace product facts, game mechanics, or the recognizable brand assets.

## Product Principles

- Keep the person → studio → project hierarchy explicit. The homepage must not read as a single-game landing page.
- Keep game mechanics findable on the dedicated game page.
- Let prospective players reach the active project and development channels without hunting.
- Preserve facts and recognizable assets while giving the studio a distinct, contemporary point of view.
- Remove archival noise instead of turning every repository artifact into public navigation.
- Treat speed, accessibility, and responsive clarity as part of the brand experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must remain keyboard accessible, screen-reader legible, usable with reduced motion, resilient under text zoom and narrow viewports, and provide sufficient contrast for all essential copy and controls.
