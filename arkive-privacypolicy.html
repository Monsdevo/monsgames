ARKIVE Privacy Policy

Publisher · Bartu Yılmaz

This policy covers the ARKIVE iPhone app. ARKIVE runs AI models on your iPhone. It requires no account. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking.

What stays on your device

ARKIVE processes your prompts, responses, attached photos, and imported documents on your iPhone. It does not send this content to the publisher or to a remote AI service. Chat history, photos attached to saved chats, imported documents and their search indexes, and app preferences are stored locally. Speech playback runs on your device and does not use the microphone.

You can optionally enter a name, date of birth, and notes in Personal Information. These details are stored on your device. ARKIVE may use your preferred name and relevant notes to personalize local replies.

If you take a photo in ARKIVE, iOS asks for camera permission. You can revoke it in iOS Settings. If you choose a photo from your library, ARKIVE receives the item you select through the system photo picker. You can also choose which files to import through the system file picker.

If you use Model Laboratory, test prompts, responses, and performance measurements may be saved on your device. You can choose to export your test results. Exported files are handled by the destination you select.

Downloads, links, and purchases

When you choose to download a model or a knowledge package, ARKIVE connects to the service hosting that file. Model downloads use Hugging Face. Knowledge downloads use the providers identified in the in-app catalog. Those providers may receive connection information such as your IP address and the requested file. ARKIVE does not include your chats, photos, profile, or imported documents in download requests. External websites you choose to open handle your visit under their own privacy policies.

Apple processes App Store purchases. ARKIVE does not receive your payment card details.

How to manage and delete local data

Local data remains in ARKIVE until you delete it or remove the app and its data from your device. You can delete chats from Conversations, model files from Local Models, and documents from Knowledge. You can edit or clear your profile fields in Personal Information and delete saved test results in Model Laboratory.

The reset action in Settings → Privacy clears chats, installed models, and preferences. Delete Knowledge documents and Model Laboratory results from their respective screens. Copies you saved outside ARKIVE can be deleted where you saved them.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. For privacy questions or requests about information you have shared with us, email bartylmaz@gmail.com. You can delete app content directly from your device using the controls described above.
