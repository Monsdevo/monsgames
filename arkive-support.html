Frequently asked questions

Can I use ARKIVE offline?

Connect to the internet for your first model download. You can also import a matching GGUF file already saved on your iPhone. Once a model is installed and selected, chat runs on your iPhone without an internet connection or an account. Any additional model, Vision component, or Knowledge source you choose to download still needs a connection.

How do I download, switch, or remove models?

Go to Settings → Local models. Check the listed file size, then tap Download. After ARKIVE verifies the file, tap Use model to switch to it. Use Delete model on an installed file to free storage. Import GGUF accepts files that match the app’s supported catalog.

How do I use Knowledge?

Go to Settings → Knowledge, choose a category, and use Import document to add a PDF, plain text, or Markdown file. You can also download catalog sources that offer a Download button. Once indexed, documents can be searched and read offline.

To use sources in a chat, open the + menu, choose Knowledge, and select the categories to search. Ask your question and open any source references in the reply to check the original material. Choose General to turn off Knowledge search for chat.

How do I ask about a photo?

In Local models, install and select Gemma 4 E2B or Gemma 4 E4B and download its matching Vision projector. Use the E2B projector for E2B and the E4B projector for E4B. In chat, open the + menu, select Photo library or Camera, attach a photo, and send your question. If the photo options are missing, check the selected model and its projector.

Troubleshooting

Responses are slow

The first reply after opening ARKIVE or switching models may take longer while the model loads. For text chat, try a smaller model such as Qwen3.5 0.8B. You can also start a new conversation and try a shorter prompt.

A model download fails

Check your connection and available iPhone storage. Keep ARKIVE open during the download. If Resume is available, tap it. Otherwise, start the download again. If verification fails after a download or import, get a fresh copy of the file.

ARKIVE reports low memory

ARKIVE may stop a reply and release the model when iOS reports low memory. If it happens again, choose a smaller model in Local models and retry in a new chat. For a Vision error, check the matching projector and try a smaller image.

The app freezes, closes, or stops mid-reply

Reopen ARKIVE and try the same action in a new chat. If a reply keeps stopping, switch to a smaller model and try again. If the issue persists, email support with the steps below.

Privacy and on-device processing

ARKIVE processes chat prompts, attached photos, and imported documents on your iPhone. This content stays out of model and Knowledge download requests. When you request a download, the hosting service can receive connection information such as your IP address.

Contact support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, ARKIVE version, the selected AI model, and what you did before the issue appeared. If you saw an error, include its wording. Leave out private chat content, documents, and photos.
