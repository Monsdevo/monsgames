How it plays

Coop on
your side

  1. Enter the location

    Explore together. Use mirrors to reveal Haunts and the anchors that bind them.

  2. Collect the evidence

    Photograph what you find. Share information and work out what to trust.

  3. Seal the anchors

    Build enough evidence to seal every anchor before time runs out.

How it fights back

Chaos on
ours

  1. Take the other side

    You’re the Haunt. Other players are investigating, and you’re working against them.

  2. Interrupt the investigation

    Flicker lights, sabotage mirrors and disrupt photographs. Make every clue a little less certain.

  3. Keep them guessing

    Mislead the Humans and protect your anchors. The clock is on your side.

Devlog 001 Development notes

The core loop worked. The ship was too big.

Our first playable blockout helped us test the investigation and sabotage loop. Moving from a ship to a motel gives that same conflict a tighter, more focused space.

The motel brings rooms, balconies and stairs around a central courtyard, with a swimming pool at its heart. It’s a location built to keep both sides close to the action.

Read the build notes

Steam lobbies have been tested on two physical Windows PCs using separate accounts. UI and music work are also underway.

Sound, onboarding, animation, feedback and final art still need work. Bad Haunts is in development; there’s no announced release date yet.
